Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. This policy covers the Onyx Beacon app for iPhone and Windows.

The short version: what you put into Onyx Beacon stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your habits, tasks, money records, writing, photos or conversations with AERO.

What the app stores, and where

Your habits, tasks, transactions, writing, AERO profile and chat history, settings and photos you add are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. Backups you export, and the automatic weekly backups, are saved in the app's Documents folder (on iPhone: Files > On My iPhone > Onyx Beacon) and go wherever you choose to send them.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your app data is included in that backup under Apple's terms, like any other app. Downloaded AI models are excluded from backups.

Permissions

Onyx Beacon asks for the camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, location, local network and notifications the first time a feature needs them. You can change any of these in the iPhone Settings app at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Reminders

Daily reminders and the comeback nudge are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server is involved.

AERO is not a professional

AERO can make mistakes. It is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser. If you are thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.

Children

Onyx Beacon is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device. Backups you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
