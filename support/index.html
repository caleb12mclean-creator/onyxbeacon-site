Onyx Beacon Plus

What do I get, and can I earn it instead?

Plus is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that opens every Plus icon and look, Month in Review and every Relight level right away. Everything except "every Relight level now" can also be earned by showing up, and Relight opens one more level each kept week. Habits, tasks, goals, money, writing, AERO, Grace, reminders and backups are always free. The Gold icon can't be bought: it's earned with the Comeback milestone.

I bought Plus but it isn't showing

Open Settings > Onyx Beacon Plus > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Plus also turns itself on when the app opens if the App Store says it's yours.

Family Sharing

If Family Sharing is on for Plus, people in your family group get it too, with no extra purchase. It appears when they open the app (or after Restore purchases).

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. In the app: Settings > Onyx Beacon Plus > Request a refund, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is approved, Plus turns off, and anything you earned yourself stays yours.

Does anything I earned ever go away?

No. Icons, looks, Month in Review and milestones stay earned even if you delete habits or goals later. Only Settings > Erase all data clears them.

Your data

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There's no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

Onyx Beacon saves an automatic backup every week, and you can make one any time in Settings. Backups are in the Files app: On My iPhone > Onyx Beacon. To move to a new phone, copy a backup to iCloud Drive (or AirDrop it), install Onyx Beacon on the new phone and restore it from Settings. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup includes the app's data as well. You can also use Wi-Fi sync between two devices on the same Wi-Fi, with a 6-digit code.

Deleting everything

Settings > Erase all data removes everything on that device. Deleting the app does the same. Backups you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

AERO

Which AERO will I get?

AERO picks the best engine your phone can run. Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence on) and AERO Lite or AERO Pro (optional downloads) all answer on the phone. AERO Lite is about 1.1 GB and suits iPhone 13 to 15; AERO Pro is about 2.5 GB, for iPhone 15 Pro and newer. Download them on Wi-Fi. Without one, AERO still gives quick answers and adds tasks, habits and goals.

Home server (optional, advanced)

If you run Ollama on a computer on your own Wi-Fi, you can point AERO at it in AERO settings for bigger answers. Your messages then go to that computer, and nowhere else.

Widgets and reminders

The widget says "Open Onyx Beacon"

Open the app once. The widget updates every time the app opens or you check something off. Widgets need iOS 17 or newer.

I'm not getting reminders

Check iPhone Settings > Notifications > Onyx Beacon, then set a time in the app's Settings > Reminders. Focus modes can hold notifications back.
